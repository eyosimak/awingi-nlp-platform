## 2. Verified Regional Landscapes & Historical Monumental Architecture

### `zengena_lake_awi.jpg`
- **Subject / Description:** Panoramic view of Lake Zengena (*Zengena Hayq*), a magnificent volcanic crater lake surrounded by dense sacred highland forests located at an elevation of ~2,500 m between Injibara and Kessa.
- **Geographical Context:** Banja Shekudad Woreda, Awi Zone, Amhara Region, Ethiopia (Coordinates: 10.9094° N, 36.9668° E).

- **Subject / Description:** Historical landscape view of Injibara town (*Kosober*), administrative and cultural capital of the Awi Zone, nestled beneath the mist and foothills of Mount Zengena.
- **Geographical Context:** Injibara Town, Awi Zone, Ethiopia.

### `lalibela_zagwe_monolith.jpg`
- **Subject / Description:** The iconic monolithic rock-hewn Church of Saint George (*Bete Giyorgis*), carved downward from living volcanic basalt in Roha (Lalibela) during the reign of monarch King Gebra Mesqel Lalibela of the Agaw Zagwe Dynasty (12th–13th c.).
- **Geographical Context:** Lalibela, Lasta Region, Ethiopia (UNESCO World Heritage Site).
- **Source URL:** [https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bet_Giyorgis_church_Lalibela_01.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bet_Giyorgis_church_Lalibela_01.jpg)

- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 Generic (`CC BY 2.0`).

## 4. Verification & Usage Standards

1. **Attribution Integrity:** Every external image retains its original author, source repository, and Creative Commons or Public Domain licensing specification.
2. **Cultural Specificity:** No image has been misattributed. Specific Agew/Awi subjects (Betty Adamu, Lake Zengena, Injibara horsemen, Agaw spinning) are explicitly marked as such; broader highland practices (monolithic stone architecture, traditional pit-loom weaving, teff threshing) are accurately credited to their respective traditions.
3. **Optimized Formats:** All assets have been sized and compressed to ensure fast delivery and accessible rendering across mobile and desktop connections.
